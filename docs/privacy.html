Paradise FLAC – Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Paradise FLAC ("the app") is an independent audio player for the Radio Paradise internet radio stream, developed by DeveshB-1. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not collect, store or share any personal information, and the developer receives no data from the app.

Network connections

To play music, the app connects directly to Radio Paradise servers:

  • stream.radioparadise.com – the audio stream
  • api.radioparadise.com – the currently playing song
  • img.radioparadise.com – album cover art

As with any internet request, these servers receive your IP address and basic request information. That is governed by Radio Paradise's own privacy policy at radioparadise.com.

Data stored on your device

The app stores one setting on your device (whether bit-perfect USB output is on). It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Internet – to stream audio and fetch song information.
  • Foreground service / wake lock – to keep playing when the screen is off.
  • Modify audio settings – to enable bit-perfect USB output when you turn it on.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
